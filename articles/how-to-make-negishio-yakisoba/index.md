I don't mind forgetting to cook rice if I have a pack of yakisoba in the fridge. I try to keep some stocked just in case.

## Ingredients

- A pack of [Maruchan Yakisoba](https://www.maruchan.co.jp/products/search/maruchanyakisoba_3ninmae.html)
- A green bell pepper
- 1/3 carrot
- 1/3 onion
- Some chopped green onion
- 150 g pork
- Salt, to taste
- Black pepper, to taste
- [Dashinomoto](https://www.ajinomoto.co.jp/products/detail/?ProductName=hondashi), to taste

## Method

1. Sear the noodles in a pan until lightly charred.
2. Remove them from the pan and stir-fry other ingredients except the green onion.
3. Return the noodles to the pan, and add the seasonings.
4. Add the green onion, then give it a quick stir.
5. That's it!

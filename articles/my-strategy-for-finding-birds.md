Sometimes, I look for birds while walking around and I bought a pair of binoculars for that. Birds live everywhere, but it's pretty hard for me to find them. Therefore, I keep in mind two things when looking for a bird I want to encounter.

- Go to a place where the bird has already been found by someone. Also, the season is an important factor, I think.
- Don't try too hard to find a specific bird; it's exhausting. Encountering a bird needs some luck, so I just visit various places over and over again.

By the way, I'm looking for "Goisagi" these days.

Since November started, it's been getting colder and there are a lot of fallen leaves near the park. I like the sound they make. Sometimes it's the wind blowing them; other times it's someone stepping on them (it sounds like snapping a chocolate bar). By the way, I haven't prepared my fall clothes yet.

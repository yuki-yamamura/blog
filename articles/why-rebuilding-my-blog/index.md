Now, I'm rebuilding my blog with Svelte including some design changes. I have two reasons:

- To have a place to post what's on my mind
- To learn a new programming language or framework, and how to use Figma for designing

One of the reasons is to make a habit of writing. Keeping it easy is the most important thing to continue a challenge, I think. And I want to try everything required, from making a design in Figma to building it with a new tech stack. In that sense, a blog is good learning material for me.

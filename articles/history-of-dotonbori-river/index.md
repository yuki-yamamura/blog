Next month, I'm going to Dotonbori for the first time after a while. In the city center, there is a canal named the Dotonbori River and I wanted to know what it looked like in the Edo era.

1. In 1612, Nariyasu Doton began building a canal connecting two rivers.
2. Tokugawa Ieyasu defeated Toyotomi Hideyoshi in the siege of Osaka, and Doton died in the battle.
3. Some people closed to Doton took over the task and finish it in 1615.
4. After a while, the canal has been called to Dotonbori river.
